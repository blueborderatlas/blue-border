# Blue Hero Research

Date: 2026-07-11

Scope: research only. This document studies real premium homepage hero patterns and recommends a direction for a future Blue hero. It does not redesign or implement the homepage.

## Research Goal

Blue needs a homepage hero that communicates within five seconds:

- Blue is a global travel platform.
- Blue helps travelers discover trusted places around the world.
- Blue is not a Dahab website.
- Blue is not a diving website.
- Blue is not a generic travel directory.

The strongest direction is a calm global montage: cities, food, trains, mountains, water, streets, people moving through places, and a small amount of diving or coast footage as one part of a wider world.

## Reference 1: One&Only Resorts

1. Brand and website name: One&Only Resorts
2. Direct URL: https://www.oneandonlyresorts.com/
3. Hero screenshot or visual description: Full-screen luxury resort imagery with strong emotional framing. The brand places large atmospheric destination visuals behind restrained copy and direct discovery paths.
4. Hero media type: Video-led/cinematic imagery. The page includes embedded video behavior and browser video fallback messaging.
5. Video editing style: Slow luxury montage. Shots feel polished, warm, destination-led and spacious. Movement is elegant rather than fast.
6. Typography style: Refined luxury serif/sans pairing. Text is minimal and positioned as a quiet overlay.
7. Navigation style: Thin, restrained top navigation with brand-first hierarchy.
8. CTA style: Minimal discovery CTA, usually short and destination-oriented.
9. Why it feels premium: It trusts the image to carry desire. There is little pressure to read.
10. What Blue can learn: Let the first frame create emotion, then let the CTA guide users into destinations.
11. What Blue should not copy: Do not copy the resort fantasy tone. Blue should feel more independent, practical and human.

2. Direct URL: https://www.sixsenses.com/

11. What Blue should not copy: Do not make the hero about wellness or luxury hospitality; Blue should feel editorial and trustworthy, not resort-sales driven.

## Pattern Comparison

Across the strongest references:

- Premium heroes use fewer words than expected.
- The first frame matters more than the headline.
- Slow motion or slow cuts feel more premium than fast travel-ad editing.
- The best global sites show diversity of place, not a collage of landmarks.
- Navigation stays available but visually quiet.
- CTAs are short and usually point to exploration.
- The hero rarely explains the whole business model.
- Trust is created through restraint, not through long claims.

For Blue, the risk is over-indexing on Dahab diving imagery. Dahab can be one clip, but it must not dominate the hero. The video should show Blue as a worldwide trust layer across many travel contexts.

## Recommended Direction for Blue

Best direction: calm global editorial montage.

The hero should feel like a short opening sequence to a travel film:

- Germany: street, train station, cafe, student or city life.
- Egypt/Dahab: one underwater or shoreline moment.
- Thailand: night market, street movement, warm light.
- Nepal: mountain road or trail moment.
- Indonesia: island, ferry, guesthouse or morning street.
- China: urban movement, food, train or neighborhood.
- Japan: quiet street, train, cafe, rain or evening.
- Italy: city street, coastline, market or station.

The video should avoid:

- Overly fast cuts.
- Drone-only travel-ad language.
- Clip after clip of famous landmarks.
- A pure diving/coastal sequence.
- Luxury resort fantasy.
- Generic "happy tourists jumping" stock-video energy.

## Recommended Hero Specifications

1. Best video duration: 15-20 seconds.
2. Best number of clips: 7-9 clips.
3. Clip length: 1.8-2.5 seconds per clip.
4. Transition style: straight cuts or very soft 6-10 frame crossfades. Avoid flashy wipes, zoom transitions or travel-vlog effects.
5. Audio default: muted by default. Do not autoplay audio.
6. Mobile fallback: static poster image using a global collage-feeling frame, ideally city/street rather than Dahab diving. Mobile may use a shorter compressed MP4/WebM or a single poster for performance.
7. Suggested hero headline: Blue
8. Suggested hero subline: Travel Begins With Trust
9. Suggested CTA: Explore Destinations
10. Recommended text placement: centered is acceptable with the current Blue identity, but the background must vary globally. If readability suffers, use centered title with low-contrast overlay and keep CTA below.
11. Recommended overlay strength: 55-70% dark overlay depending on footage. Use stronger vignette near text and softer darkness at edges so footage remains visible.
